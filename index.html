"Fait Main" is my first major learning project, produced with Télé Vaudoise. For this series, I contacted ten Swiss artisans, each of whom shared their expertise through a three-minute episode.

This project was entirely conceived and produced by me — from organizing the shoots to editing, including the complete graphic design of the show.

A wood imitation incorporating a DNA imprint to evoke the authenticity and uniqueness of handmade craftsmanship in the logo.

Created with After Effects, the opening credits feature several interesting effects that mimic organic motion, with dynamic transitions, reveals, and sound effects.

“Nature Morte” was a photography project created to explore light manipulation and the use of Photoshop.

“Givenchy” is a logo animation for the Givenchy brand, showcasing my skills in After Effects.

As an aspiring interactive media designer, I am constantly seeking new experiences that challenge and expand my creative horizons.

I thrive in environments that allow me to explore diverse artistic styles and perspectives, always pushing the boundaries of design.

Music serves as one of my greatest sources of inspiration, influencing my approach to visual storytelling and interactive experiences.
